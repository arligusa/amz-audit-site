Title front-loads brand/style before the search-driving attribute. Shoppers scanning results on mobile often only see the first 60-80 characters before truncation.
Fix: reorder to brand → capacity → material → category keyword → differentiator.
A mid-list bullet leads with a feature, not a benefit. "Push-button locking spout" doesn't tell a shopper why it matters to them.
Fix: lead with the benefit — "Leak-proof for your bag or car" — then the mechanism.
No dedicated size/scale image in the gallery. "Is this actually as big as I think" is one of the most common pre-purchase hesitations.
Fix: add one size-comparison image with capacity called out in large text.

Review volume is strong but not contextualized on-page. Star ratings are easy to glance past without a specific, quotable reinforcement.
Fix: add a social-proof-forward image or A+ module surfacing a credible pattern from reviews.
Care/compatibility questions aren't pre-answered. Predictable questions either slow-burn in public Q&A or become return reasons.
Fix: add a short FAQ-style A+ module for the 3-4 most predictable questions.
